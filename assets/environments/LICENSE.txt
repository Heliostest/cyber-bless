Additional optional developer tree forms (2026-11-05)
Just a simple tree cypress/willow/pine outputs use the same pinned MIT code.
The cypress source describes Italian cypress. The source key pine is a
fir-like tiered conifer, not a species-certified Japanese garden pine.
Full source parameters, names and limitations: trees/simple-tree/README.md.

The two cherry outputs are product art adaptations of the same MIT source
oak/wood branching and foliage clump/AO pipeline, with spreading crowns,
flatter clumps and a new procedural five-petal flower atlas and pink palette.
They are not an upstream built-in cherry species or cultivar identification.
No external Sakura Tree 01 geometry, textures or code are included in them.
Complete MIT notice and provenance: trees/simple-tree/LICENSE.txt and README.md.

Chinese toon (toona), green maple (acer), and autumn maple (maple) are
additional product art adaptations of the same pinned MIT oak/wood and
foliage pipeline. Each has two deterministic variants, distinct branch
parameters and a product-authored procedural leaf atlas. These are not
native upstream botanical generators or certified cultivar models.
No botanical reference photographs or third-party textures are included.
Parameters, extension hashes and provenance: trees/simple-tree/README.md.

Timber furniture and shallow water are original project geometry/shaders.
Evaluated external references are documented in furniture/PROVENANCE.md and
water/README.md; those reference implementations/assets are not redistributed.
